Grass-only optimization build
=============================

Branch: grass-only-optimizations
Base: dev at c60554d2; upstream MT rendering baseline: 871e2491.

Keeps the five grass/detail shader overrides and their DX10/DX11 engine support:
hardware instancing, batching, once-per-frame instance packing, depth sorting,
and distance-based density controls.

Restores upstream AO/IL blur, SSR scaling, and screen-space postprocessing
render-target copies. Removes SSFX_AO_SCALED_BLUR and both custom AO shaders.
AO and SSR remain available through the installed Screen Space Shaders mod.

Run deploy.bat to build and install all four MT executables and the five shaders.
Deployment also removes ssfx_ao.ps and ssfx_ao_blur.ps from the shader patch mod.
When manually updating, replace the old shader patch instead of merging it;
remove those two obsolete overrides from any prior loose installation as well.
Clear the Anomaly shader cache before launching.

docs/OPTIMIZATION_AUDIT.md describes the earlier combined optimization branch,
not the screen-space behavior of this grass-only branch.
